UTTER — PRODUCT HUNT DETAILS

TAGLINE
Private, on-device voice dictation for every Mac app.

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Utter is a native macOS menu-bar app that turns speech into text wherever your
cursor is. Hold a key, speak, and release—the result is typed into Mail, Slack,
Notes, a browser, a terminal, or almost any other Mac app.

Speech recognition runs locally with whisper.cpp. The default model ships with
the app, so everyday dictation works offline with no account, cloud processing,
telemetry, subscription, API key, or usage limit.

WHY UTTER
Most dictation tools send your voice to a server. Utter is designed around a
simpler promise: your recordings, transcripts, notes, history, and custom words
stay on your Mac.

CURRENT FEATURES

- Push-to-talk anywhere: hold Fn, Right Option, Right Command, or Right Control;
  speak; then release to transcribe into the active app.
- A native main workspace with dedicated Dictation, Dictionary, Snippets,
  Transforms, Notes, and Companion sections.
- Hands-free dictation: start from a keyboard shortcut, the floating pill, or by
  tapping Space while holding the dictation key. Recording stops after silence.
- Fully on-device speech recognition using bundled Whisper models.
- Speech-model selector with speed, memory, and accuracy guidance for your Mac.
- Live microphone level, elapsed time, status, transcript preview, and useful
  errors inside a compact floating pill.
- Context-aware formatting that adapts punctuation and tone for chat, email,
  documents, and code without changing the words you spoke.
- Optional sentence-structure repair and spoken punctuation.
- Optional surrounding-text hints for better continuity and recognition. Context
  is processed locally, never saved, and skipped in password fields.
- Custom dictionary for names, acronyms, product names, and technical terms.
- Voice snippets: speak a trigger phrase to insert reusable text with dynamic
  date, time, day, and clipboard placeholders.
- On-device voice transforms for selected or recently dictated text: make it
  professional, concise, casual, formal, shorter, longer, polite, or direct;
  translate, fix grammar, summarize, and draft replies.
- Custom voice commands that can replace or append transformed text.
- Built-in notes: dictate directly into Utter, create or continue notes by voice,
  search notes, edit titles, and export individual notes to Markdown.
- On-device note actions including summarize, extract action items, clean up
  dictation, format as a document, and suggest a title.
- Meeting transcription that captures your microphone and call audio, processes
  both locally, and saves the transcript as “You” and “Others.”
- Optional call detection that offers to transcribe but never records until the
  user explicitly accepts.
- The call-transcription offer expands spatially from the floating pill and
  returns to it when dismissed, preserving the relationship between both states.
- Searchable dictation history with word count, dictation count, streaks,
  reinsertion, and Markdown, CSV, or plain-text export. History can be disabled.
- Clipboard-safe insertion that preserves existing rich text, images, and files.
- Optional animated desktop companion that reacts to dictation, microphone
  levels, cursor movement, and typing speed without reading what you type.
- Smoothly draggable companion with multiple sizes, cat sounds, stretch and
  water reminders, Pomodoro timing, pinned notes, and custom character packs.
- Native macOS windows, controls, materials, light/dark appearance, menu-bar
  operation, optional Dock icon, launch at login, and Reduce Motion support.
- Dedicated General, Speech, Appearance, Privacy, Diagnostics, and About settings
  using native macOS forms, toggles, radio groups, and sidebar navigation.
- Local persistent storage with atomic writes and recovery protection for notes
  and history.
- Open-source under the Apache 2.0 license.

PRIVACY

- Audio and text processing happen on the Mac.
- No account or sign-in.
- No telemetry or advertising.
- No cloud API keys.
- No uploaded recordings or transcripts.
- No usage limits.
- Network access is only needed when the user chooses to download an optional
  speech model.

REQUIREMENTS

- macOS 13.3 or later.
- Apple silicon or Intel Mac.
- Microphone and Accessibility permission for global dictation.
- Meeting capture requires additional system-audio or Screen Recording access.
- AI text transforms require macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence enabled; core
  dictation works without it.
- Current beta builds are signed but not yet notarized.

ROADMAP DIRECTION
These are planned directions, not shipped features or committed release dates:

- Notarized distribution and a simpler update experience.
- Broader multilingual dictation and improved language controls.
- Better chronological meeting transcripts and optional remote-speaker
  separation.
- More precise browser-call detection and meeting-source labeling.
- Customizable hands-free and note-capture shortcuts.
- Hash-verified, resumable speech-model downloads and easier model updates.
- More companion characters, behaviors, animation states, and authoring tools.
- Richer note organization, bulk export, backup, and restore.

MAKER NOTE
I built Utter because I wanted fast Mac dictation without sending my voice,
documents, or selected text to a server. It started as a simple push-to-talk
utility and grew into an on-device workspace for dictation, reusable snippets,
voice-powered text editing, notes, meetings, and a small desktop companion.

The goal is not to replace the keyboard. It is to make speaking a private,
reliable input method that works naturally alongside it.
