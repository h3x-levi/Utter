# Companion sound provenance

`cat-meow.wav`, `cat-meow-double.wav`, `cat-purr.wav`, `cat-purr-warm.wav`,
and `cat-trill.wav` are original synthesized assets generated by
`scripts/generate_cat_sounds.py`. They contain no third-party recordings and
require no external attribution or redistribution license.

The following cues were supplied by the project owner from Pixabay and converted
to mono PCM WAV with short fades and level matching. The raw MP3 downloads are
not bundled:

- `cat-meow-natural.wav` — “Cute Cat Meow,” Dragon Studio,
  source download `dragon-studio-cute-cat-meow-472372.mp3`.
- `cat-purr-natural.wav` — “Purring Cat,” Universfield,
  https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/animals-purring-cat-156459/
- `dog-bark.wav` — “Free Dog Bark,” Dragon Studio,
  source download `dragon-studio-free-dog-bark-419014.mp3`.
- `dog-pant.wav` — “Tired Dog Panting 52,” mbardak,
  source download `mbardak-tired-dog-panting-52-535174.mp3`.

The downloads identify `https://pixabay.com/` and Pixabay’s CDN as their source.
They are used under the Pixabay Content License in a larger application, not
redistributed as standalone audio files:
https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/
